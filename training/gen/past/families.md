# Holidays with relative modifiers (round of 2026-11-21)

Goal: phrases a person types or writes that name a holiday with a word that picks which occurrence: the past one ("last purim"), the coming one ("next diwali"), or this one. This file lists the kinds of phrasing we already expect. It shows no output formats. Unusual real phrasings matter more than many copies of the common ones.

## Holidays in scope

Any holiday a person might name. Cover all of these groups, each at least 15% of your phrases:
- Fixed-date holidays: Christmas, Christmas Eve, New Year's Day and Eve, Halloween, Valentine's Day, St Patrick's Day, Independence Day, Canada Day, Boxing Day, Guy Fawkes, Anzac Day, Juneteenth and others.
- Moving holidays on a weekday: Thanksgiving (US and Canada), Memorial Day, Labor Day, Mother's Day, Father's Day, MLK Day, Presidents Day, Black Friday, Cyber Monday, bank holidays.
- Easter and the days around it: Easter, Good Friday, Easter Monday, Palm Sunday, Ash Wednesday, Mardi Gras, Pentecost.
- Lunar and lunisolar holidays: Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn, Dragon Boat, Diwali, Holi, Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Passover, Hanukkah, Purim.

## Families

| Code | Family | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| A | "last" and a holiday | "last purim", "last christmas", "last easter" |
| B | Other past words | "previous thanksgiving", "this past halloween", "the most recent eid", "the last diwali" |
| C | A past holiday with a time or part of the day | "last thanksgiving at 4pm", "last diwali evening" |
| D | A past holiday as a bound or in a range | "since last christmas", "from last easter to now", "between last passover and today" |
| E | A past holiday inside a sentence or message | "the photos from last hanukkah", "we met at the party last halloween" |
| F | The coming one | "next purim", "this diwali", "the coming easter", "this year's thanksgiving", "the upcoming eid" |
| G | Relative years and counts | "christmas last year", "last year's diwali", "thanksgiving next year", "the christmas before last", "two easters ago" |
| H | Near misses | the same words with another meaning: "last day of hanukkah", "the last night of passover", "last minute christmas shopping", "the last christmas of the decade", "last christmas" as a song title, "last friday" |
| I | Typos, abbreviations, voice, odd spacing | "lst xmas", "last thanksgivng", "prev hannukah", "last ramdan" |
| J | Anything else with these meanings | constructions that fit none of the above; add a short note |

## Variation to cover

- Context words: "remind me", "photos from", "since", "out of office", "expense report for", "the party", "trip", "deadline".
- Registers: terse quick-add, voice transcript, casual chat, email, forum post, non-native English.
- Regions: US, UK, Ireland, Canada, Australia, India, Malaysia, Singapore, Israel, and others.
