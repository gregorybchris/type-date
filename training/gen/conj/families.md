# Conjunction families (round of 2026-11-21)

Goal: phrases where one recurrence joins several parts with "and", plus misspelled and abbreviated weekday and recurrence words. This file lists families and phrasings only. It never shows output formats.

## Families

| Code | Family | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| A | Nth weekdays, different weekdays, one recurrence | "every third tuesday and second monday of each month", "1st & 3rd mon, 2nd fri monthly", "second tuesday and fourth thursday of every month", "first and last friday and third monday of the month" |
| A+ | Same with a time or a bound | "first monday and third friday of every month at 3pm", "2nd and 4th tuesdays, last friday monthly until june", "every 1st wed and 3rd thu through 2027" |
| B | Own time per weekday | "tuesdays at 1:30 and thursdays at 3", "mon 9-10, wed 2-3:30 every week", "every tuesday at 9am and thursday at 2pm", "tues 7pm & thurs 6pm weekly" |
| B+ | Same with ranges, durations, 24h, noon, bounds, every other week | "mondays 9am for 1 hour and wednesdays 2pm for 30 min", "tue 1400-1530 thu 1000-1130", "every other tuesday at 9 and friday at noon until dec 20", "mon at noon, fri at 5, for 6 weeks" |
| C | Contrast: shared time vs own times | "tuesdays and thursdays at 3", "tuesdays at 3 and thursdays at 3", "every monday and wednesday at 9 and friday at 10" |
| D | Other joined weekday recurrences | "every monday and every friday", "mon, wed, fri every week", "weekdays except wednesday and friday", "every tuesday and thursday in march" |
| E | Misspelled or abbreviated weekday and recurrence words | "every thrus", "every wends", "evry tusday", "thurs", "tues/thurs 3pm", "mons and weds at 9", "wkdays", "ea. monday", "mon+wed 10am", "M/W/F 9", "TR 2pm" |
| F | Near misses and negatives | one time only ("mon 9am and wed 2pm" with no recurrence cue), different frequencies in one phrase ("every tuesday and every other friday"), nth weekdays with their own times ("first monday at 9 and third friday at 2"), non-dates ("call mom and dad at 5", "2nd and 3rd floor") |

## Variation to cover

- Ordinals: first, 1st, one, second ... fifth, last, "first and last", "2nd & 4th".
- Weekday forms: full, 3 letters, tues, thurs, weds, plural, single letters, codes (M T W R F), typos.
- Joiners: and, &, +, comma, slash, semicolon, "then", none.
- Time forms: 9am, 9:00, 0900, 9 o'clock, nine thirty, noon, ranges (9-10, 9 to 10am), "for 90 min", 24h.
- Bounds: until, through, for N weeks or months, starting, "this semester", "through 2027".
- Register: terse quick-add, voice transcript, formal notice, casual text, non-native English, UK, AU, US, IN.
- Surrounding words: event names ("piano lessons", "office hours", "standup"), filler ("can we do", "please").
