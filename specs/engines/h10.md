# h10: h9 behind a rewrite step for an hour with a missing a or p

Code: `packages/baselines/src/h10.ts`, rules in `src/h10/rewrite.ts`. h9 is called unchanged.

Why: "yesterday at 9m to tomorrow 3pm" (9am with the "a" dropped) was read as `dt -1d 9?`. The unknown word `9m` ended the parse and the whole "to tomorrow 3pm" tail was lost, so the picker showed only the start date.

Rule: a standalone `H` or `H:MM` followed by `m` (not `mo`, `min`, `9 m`, or `9m2`) keeps the hour and drops the `m`. The hour then has an unknown meridiem, as a bare `9` has. With a range such as "9m to 3pm" the rest of the phrase decides.

Not covered: other dropped letters in meridiems (`9a.`, `9 a`), minutes typed as `m`.
