# h16: lunar and lunisolar holidays, in front of h15.

## Method

- Request (2026-11-21): support lunar holidays: Lunar New Year, Mid-Autumn, Dragon Boat, Diwali, Holi, Ramadan, Eid al-Fitr, Eid al-Adha, Rosh Hashanah, Yom Kippur, Passover, Hanukkah, Purim. Policy and the new DSL form: `specs/dsl.md`, "Lunar and lunisolar holidays".
- New core form `L:CODE±N` and `L:CODE@YYYY±N`, a single day. Dates come from a table, `packages/core/src/lunar-table.ts`, generated by `training/gen/lunar/make_tables.py` from the `holidays` package (0.83). Coverage: Chinese and Hebrew 2000-2100, Islamic (Umm al-Qura) 2000-2077, Diwali and Holi (India) 2001-2035. A date outside the table resolves to nothing.
- Date checks: the Hebrew dates match convertdate's arithmetic calendar (the script stops on a mismatch). Against ICU (`Intl`, Node ICU 77): Mid-Autumn and Dragon Boat agree everywhere. Lunar New Year differs in 2027 and 2030; for 2027 the table gives Feb 6 and ICU gives Feb 7. The Islamic dates agree up to 2030 and differ by a day in 18 or 19 years after that. This is why the dates come from a table and not from `Intl`.
- Engine `packages/baselines/src/h16.ts`, rule in the lazy chunk `src/h16/lunar.ts`. h1 to h15 are unchanged.
  - Names: a table of names, spellings, transliterations and typos per holiday. It also covers Chinese, Hebrew and Arabic script for some holidays, and named days ("kol nidre", "chand raat", "choti diwali", "first seder", "lantern festival").
  - Each holiday phrase is replaced by a sentinel date (`1998-12-31`, `1998-12-30`, `1998-12-29`) that h15 reads. The sentinel in h15's answer becomes the token, so h15 still reads the time, range or bound around it ("until eid", "diwali at 7pm", "from purim to passover").
  - Ramadan, Hanukkah and Passover alone (or with "during", "over", "all 8 days of") are a range. Eves, "erev", "Nth night", "Nth day", "first N days" and "last day of ramadan" become offsets.
  - A year after the name gives absolute dates. The year may be Gregorian, Hijri ("ramadan 1448") or Hebrew ("rosh hashanah 5788"), in digits, `'27`, `2k27`, or words.
  - Guards return `x` for past occurrences ("last diwali"), recurrences, greetings and things named after the holiday. A fuzzy period ("diwali week") is `x` unless the text has another date ("diwali weekend nov 7-9").
- Picker: the built-in parser is h16 (`packages/picker/src/parse.ts`).

## Data (round of 2026-11-21)

Briefs are in `training/gen/lunar/`: `families.md` (families A to L, every holiday), the writer brief and the labeler brief. There were four writer sets:
- w1: terse quick-add and chat, for the open pool.
- w2: voice and non-native English, for the open pool.
- w3: messages and notices by region, for the sealed set.
- w4: 243 spans copied from public pages, for the sealed set. Sources: duckling, Stack Exchange, Hacker News, GitHub issues, school, office and government calendars, news.

Overlap: the two pool sets shared 37 short phrases ("ramadan", "purim"), which were merged. w3 and w4 shared 1 row, w3 and the pool 2, w4 and the pool 3. `td data drop-seen` against all data, evals and the pool writer files dropped 4. Two independent labelers per set, then `td data consensus`:

| Set | Inputs | Agree | Unsure | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pool (w1, w2) | 536 | 519 | 63 | 453, `data/pool/lunar/dev_p01.jsonl` |
| sealed (w3, w4) | 546 | 505 | 84 | 419: e18 `test_lunar_writers` (238) and `test_lunar_web` (181) |

Stale rows: none. The 4 e17 rows and 2 pool rows that name one of these holidays carry an explicit date ("diwli 12 nov off"), and the label keeps that date. So e18 is e17 plus the two splits.

## Results

lunar pool (`scripts/pools.ts --only lunar`, resolved comparison): h15 0.119 even half, 0.133 odd half; h16 0.943 even, 0.934 odd. The odd half is not held out, because I read all pool texts while writing the rule.

Regression gate (`scripts/pools.ts h16 --compare h15`, every open pool row): 371 gains, 3 regressions, 0 known conflicts. One was fixed ("holi festival of colours tickets"). The other two are in the lunar pool's odd half, whose failures stay hidden, and are not fixed. Earlier runs found and fixed one more: "diwali weekend nov 7-9" (a fuzzy period made the whole text `x`).

Size (eval.ts gzip): 28.9 kB eager (+0.2 kB against h15), 49.7 kB lazy (+3.8 kB). The date table is 4.0 kB raw, 2.7 kB gzip. Median latency 0.40 ms (h15 0.39 ms).

Tests: `packages/baselines/test/h16.test.ts` (59 phrases and resolution on the reference day). `packages/core/test/core.test.ts` covers parse, serialize and resolve of `L:CODE±N`, including table edges and a Ramadan that falls twice in one Gregorian year.

## Not covered

- Spellings and regional names not in the table (families I and J are the weakest on both sealed splits).
- A holiday named twice in one text ("holi khelne chalo holi morning 10 baje"), or a list after it ("eid ul fitr and the day after"). h15 does not make a list from the sentinel and an anchor.
- "dragon boat to end of june": h15 drops "end of june" after a sentinel date.
- Past occurrences ("last diwali"): `x`, because there is no past form.
- Holidays not in the table: Sukkot, Shavuot, Navratri, Dussehra, Vesak, the other days of the five-day Diwali festival, and Hebrew months and dates ("14th of adar").
- Observed-date differences: Islamic dates by country (Umm al-Qura only), Tet and Seollal (the Chinese table), Passover in Israel (7 days, the label uses 8).
