# h11: general readers in front of h10 for named days and holidays, spoken numbers and clock idioms, numeric formats, weekday codes, deadline words and frequency words.

## Method

- Engine `packages/baselines/src/h11.ts` (eager, 85 bytes gzip more than h10). Rules in `src/h11/` (lazy chunk loaded by `loadH11()`, which also loads the h9 chunk). Without `loadH11`, `parseH11` is h10. h1..h10 are unchanged except one re-export in `src/h9/all.ts` (`clean`).
- Order in `src/h11/all.ts`: h9 `clean` and h8 `normalize` on the lowercased text -> `words` (spoken numbers, years, military times, clock idioms, part of day) -> `rewrite` (formats, small text fixes) -> direct rules that return a DSL line (24/7, bare `N-M`, frequency words, periods, `from now`, dotted dates, monthly lists, end-of-day deadlines, weekday range, weekday codes, corrections, opening and closing words) -> placeholder step (weekday of a relative week, holidays) -> h10 -> post fixes (`fix`) -> deadline length rule. When no rule rewrites the text, h10 gets the original text.
- The placeholder step replaces a phrase h10 cannot write (a holiday, `friday of next week`) by an ISO date with year 2080+ or 2090+, lets h10 parse the rest (times, offsets, ranges, "the day before ..."), and puts the DSL form back. If the placeholder does not survive the parse, h11 falls back to h10.
- New DSL forms: `E±N` (Easter-relative day) and `WD@W±N` (weekday of a relative week). Implemented in `packages/core` (types, `parseDsl`, `serializeDsl`, `resolveDsl`; tests in `test/core.test.ts`). Documented in `specs/dsl.md` ("Easter-relative dates, weekday inside a relative week"). The picker needed no change (it renders resolved dates); one test covers both forms.
- Policies decided and written in `specs/dsl.md`: numeric date order, deadline words, named days table, weekday codes, bare durations, frequency words, other label decisions.
- Development: even lines of `persona/dev` and `web/dev7..dev9`; odd lines are an internal validation set (scored, failing rows never printed, never fitted). No sealed file was read. `evals/e12` was scored once, aggregate only; `runs/h11/errors/e12.jsonl` was not read.

## Rules (what each covers)

| Module | Covers |
|---|---|
| `holidays.ts` | One table of 76 entries (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ, India fixed days, Easter family) as `MM-DD`, `wd#K@MM`, `E±N`, with `~±Nd/w` for Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Election Day, Victoria Day. A stated year gives the concrete date. "every thanksgiving" is a recurrence. A holiday next to another date is a label. Fuzzy periods (week, break, season) are left to h10. |
| `words.ts` | Years ("nineteen ninety nine", "two thousand and five"), ordinals next to a weekday or month, hour words after at/by/around, military and spoken 24h times ("0900 hours", "1745h", "08.30 hrs", "oh seven hundred", "eighteen forty five"), minute idioms ("half past 11", "quarter of five", "five of three", "twenty five past nine", "five and twenty past four", "ten to noon", "noon thirty"), part of day before an hour or a bound, `10 o'clock in the night`. |
| `formats.ts` | ISO week date, ISO ordinal date, ISO year-month, ISO and basic ISO date-time (T means a 24h time), Roman-numeral months. |
| `rules.ts` | Weekday codes ("MWF 10-10:50", "t/th 2pm", "tu+th 7:15am"), weekday of a relative week and its ranges, British "friday week" and "monday fortnight", `from now until X`, frequency words, "all of last month", "within 3 days", corrections ("tuesday no wednesday"), duration before the start ("block 90 minutes on tuesday starting at 10", "7p 1h"), opening and closing words ("opens 1 january and closes 31 january"), arrival and departure, monthly day lists ("1,2,3 of month"), nth weekday in a list of months, end-of-day deadlines. |
| `post.ts` | Text rewrites ("the 5 of next month", "month end", "eom", "only on saturday and sunday", "every tuesday fortnight", "every 14 february", "at 23", "midnight tonight", "tn 9p", "tom 2pm", fillers, "&" lists, "21st, 22nd and 23rd of may", numeric end year copied to the start, "q4 2027", "first to last day of 2008", fuzzy period words next to explicit dates) and output fixes ("through june" = last day, "every october" = month filter, count for "every 2 weeks ... for 6 weeks", business-day and "following" anchors, `until the end of next month` open end, Monday-first day lists, deadline length rule). |

## Costs

- Eager gzip 22.12 kB (h10 22.03 kB). Lazy chunk 27.9 kB gzip (h10 16.4 kB, so +11.5 kB: the holiday table and word readers are about half). The first build had 27.2 kB eager because `h9/rules` became a shared chunk; fixed by loading `clean` through the h9 lazy entry.
- Node latency over 2,441 pool texts through `predict`: p50 0.25 ms, p95 0.71 ms, p99 1.16 ms (h10: 0.14, 1.01, 1.63). Not measured on a phone.
- `validate-data.ts` on h11 predictions for all 66,058 pool texts: 0 bad.
- Tests: `test/h11.test.ts` (about 235 cases), core 217, baselines 784, picker 42; `tsc --noEmit` clean in core, baselines, picker.

## Remaining clusters worth doing next

- Weekday codes and `for the next N weeks`: decide by relabeling the older tax rows, then flip two rules (about 30 rows).
- Short "by X" rows labelled as the date in the real pools (30 rows): needs a cue that separates them from terse open rows, or a relabel.
- Recurrence bounds: `starting in may for 10 occurrences`, `except sundays` with a year bound (`uY@2027 sY@2027` vs expanded dates), `since march`, `past 5 weekends`.
- Number and weekday words with typos in clock idioms (`quater past siks`, `haff past too`): needs the h8 normalizer to know the idiom words.
- Dates with a weekday word between them in a message (`Fri 5/28 - Mon 5/31`, arrival and departure) beyond the cases covered.
- Two or three dates in one message where the label is the first or the "next" one (`Next attempt: Fri 10/30`).
- Orthodox Easter, lunar holidays, Nth weekend of a month.
