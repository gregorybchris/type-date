# h12: conjunctions of recurrences and start/until bounds in front of h11.

## Method

- Engine `packages/baselines/src/h12.ts` (eager, 69 bytes gzip more than h11). Rules in `src/h12/` (lazy chunk loaded by `loadH12()`, which also loads the h11 chunk). Without `loadH12`, `parseH12` is h11. h1..h11 are unchanged.
- Order in `src/h12/multi.ts`: `unsupported` (returns `x`) -> `registrar` (weekday codes with a time) -> `bareStart` -> `nthConjunction` -> `dayTimes` -> `withBounds` -> h11 on the original text (or on the rewritten text when `rewrite` changed it) -> `fixOut`. Every rule parses the shared part of the phrase with h11 and puts the multi-part form back.
- New DSL forms (see `specs/dsl.md`, "Conjunctions of recurrences" and "Start clauses"): `WD@TIME` list per weekday (`dt R w/1 tu@13:30,th@15:00`), nth lists with different weekdays (`d R m/1 mo#2,tu#3`). Implemented in `packages/core` (parse, serialize, resolve, tests). The picker shows one chip per weekday and edits one weekday at a time (`packages/picker`).

| Module | Covers |
|---|---|
| `nth.ts` | Several nth weekdays joined by and, &, /, +, n, comma: "every third tuesday and second monday of each month", "1st & 3rd saturday monthly", "2nd wednesdays". Ordinal typos (seccond, forth). The list is written in weekday order. |
| `days.ts` | A time per weekday ("tuesdays at 1:30 and thursdays at 3", "mon 6pm for 90 min and fri 12pm for 45 min"), weekdays sharing a time inside one clause, military times, 24h context. Registrar codes ("MW 10:50 AM 12:10 PM", "TR 8 - 9:15 am", "M-W-F 3pm weekly"; a bare code is a date list). A lowercase path with a whitelist of registrar codes, because the picker lowercases its input. |
| `bounds.ts` | Start and until clauses on any recurrence: the clause is cut out, h11 parses the skeleton, each operand is parsed alone (month, date, month and year, year, weekday, relative week or month, "the end of ...", "the first of june") and written as `s` or `u`. h11's own output is kept when it has the same bounds. "starting in june" alone is the month. |
| `unsupported.ts` | `x` for nth weekdays with their own times and for different frequencies in one phrase. |
| `post.ts` | Rewrites (bi weekly, wk day, "ever", "mo.", "twice a week mon and thu", weekday ranges with a cue, "every other monday and friday", "every X and every Y", "at 1930") and output fixes (plural weekday list is a recurrence, week count times weekdays, month filters from "except june, july", "(january, march)" and "every even month"). |

## Data (round of 2026-11-21)

Briefs in `training/gen/conj/` (`families.md`, writer and labeler briefs). Two web rounds (`web-10`: institutional schedules, 288 phrases; `web-11`: scheduling tools, parser tests, forum posts, 517), eight LLM writer sets, two independent labelers per set, `td data consensus`. Pool halves in `data/pool/conj/dev_*` (w1 casual, w2 admin, w4 start clauses, w6 varied bounds, web 10). Sealed splits `test_conj_writers` (602 rows: w5 start clauses, w7 varied bounds, w8 conjunction families A to F) and `test_conj_web` (289 rows: web 11) in `evals/e13` (parent e12; see its manifest `known_issues`).

- Writer 3 was dropped: 231 of its 269 consensus rows had the same text as writer 1. Writer 8 replaced it with an isolation rule in the brief.
- `policy_flags` were narrowed for new sources (plural weekdays are a recurrence cue, per-weekday time rows skip the meridiem flag, four-digit military times are stated). Rows already seen in the pool, e12 or training data were removed from the sealed splits (`td data drop-seen`).
- Real web pages almost never show a time per weekday in one phrase (writers produce those) and show many forms the DSL cannot write: alternatives ("M or W"), month parity as prose, recurrences tied to another event, nth-last other than last.

## Costs

Eager gzip 22.18 kB (h11 22.12 kB). Lazy chunk 33.5 kB gzip (h11 27.9 kB, so +5.6 kB). Node latency p50 0.30 ms on the e13 rows (h11 0.30 ms). Not measured on a phone. Tests: `test/h12.test.ts` (49 cases), baselines 833, core 222, picker 55.

## Not done

- The sealed `test_conj_*` splits are burned for h12 tuning. Further rule work needs a new independent set.
- Forms with no DSL: alternatives, month parity as prose, recurrence tied to another event, nth-last other than last, every-N-months with ordinals, several times for one weekday, "twice a week" without days.
- "every third wednesday" (ordinal after "every" with no month cue) is still ambiguous: h11 writes the monthly form, the policy flag drops it from evals.
- A first date in the past ("every other wed starting last week") shows as the first date in the picker. Whether to show it is a display decision.
- Month-only bounds from old synthetic labels (41 v007 rows) disagree with the new policy. Relabel or drop them in the next dataset version.
