# h17: past and counted holidays, in front of h16.

## Method

- Request (2026-11-21): "last purim" gave `x`; holidays should take relative modifiers like exact dates ("last dec 25" is `12-25<`). A follow-up asked about "two channukah's ago"; the user chose to add occurrence counts in the same round. Policy: `specs/dsl.md`, "Past holidays".
- Core changes:
  - The past marker `<` is now valid on an nth weekday of a named month (`th#4@11<`), on Easter (`E±N<`) and on a lunar holiday (`L:CODE±N<`). An anchor takes it on its base (`th#4@11<~+1d`).
  - Occurrence counts are new: `<N` is the Nth most recent and `^N` the Nth upcoming, with N from 2 to 10. They are valid on those forms and on `MM-DD`.
  - One old core test listed `fr#1@03<` as invalid; it is valid now.
- Engine `packages/baselines/src/h17.ts`, rule in the lazy chunk `src/h17/past.ts`. h1 to h16 are unchanged.
  - Past words: "last", "previous", "this past", "the past", "this last", "most recent", "the latest", "recent", "prior", "preceding", "just gone" and typos. Words after the holiday also count ("christmas just gone", "passover that just ended").
  - Method: the past word is removed, h16 parses the rest, and the first holiday token in its answer is marked. This happens only when h16 ignored the past word: no reading, the same reading, a reading without a holiday ("last good friday" as `fr-1`), the same holiday without a marker, or fewer holidays.
  - The words right after the past word must name a holiday on their own, so "last call ... dec 2" and "last train on new years eve" are left alone.
  - Several past words in one phrase mark several tokens ("between last eid and last diwali").
  - Counts: "N Xs ago", "N Xs back", "a couple of Xs ago" (`<N`); "the X before last" and "last to last X" (`<2`); "the X after next" (`^2`). The holiday is found in the words before the keyword, singular or plural.
  - A holiday with a relative year ("christmas 2 yrs ago", "mother's day last year", "last year's diwali") is `x`, by policy.
- Picker: the built-in parser is h17 (`packages/picker/src/parse.ts`).

## Data (round of 2026-11-21)

Briefs are in `training/gen/past/` (families A to J, all holiday groups). There were two writer sets of 160 each:
- w1: quick-add, chat and voice, for the open pool.
- w2: messages and notices by region, for the sealed set.

The two writers returned identical per-family counts because both followed the brief's percentages exactly. They shared only 7 texts. `td data drop-seen` dropped 4 rows from w1 and 8 from w2 (w2 was also checked against w1 and the lunar writer sets).

Stale rows: `scripts/stale-past.ts` lists rows where h17 answers with a past marker or a count on a holiday token and the label resolves differently. It found 5 rows in the lunar pool and 4 in e18, all labeled `x` before.

Two independent labelers labeled all four files. `training/gen/past/apply.py` runs consensus and applies the result:

| Set | Inputs | Agree | Unsure | Kept |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| pool (w1) | 156 | 154 | 8 | 146, `data/pool/past/dev_p01.jsonl` |
| sealed (w2) | 152 | 149 | 20 | 128, e19 `test_past` |
| stale, lunar pool | 5 | 5 | 0 | 5 relabeled in place |
| stale, e18 | 4 | 4 | 0 | 4 relabeled in e19 |

## Results

past pool (`scripts/pools.ts --only past`, resolved comparison): h16 0.219 even half, 0.178 odd half; h17 0.849 even, 0.726 odd. The odd half is not held out, because I read all pool texts while writing the rule.

Regression gate (`scripts/pools.ts h17 --compare h16`, every open pool row): 94 gains, 3 regressions. Two are visible and both are policy edge cases the labelers marked `x`: "last st paddys" (a bare saint's name without "day", the h11 cut-off rule) and "previous seder night". One is in the past pool's odd half and stays hidden. Earlier runs found and fixed "Last call for pre-orders: Dec 2" (a past word before a date that is not a holiday name).

e19, exact, first and only run of the frozen engine:

| Split | n | h16 | h17 |
|---|---|---|---|
| test_past | 128 | 0.180 | 0.703 |
| all of e19 | 18,035 | 0.9118 | 0.9157 |

Against h16 on e19: 70 rows fixed (67 in test_past, 3 in the lunar splits, all relabeled rows), 0 broken.

By family (test_past): A 0.80, B 0.75, C 1.00, D 0.62, E 0.81, F 0.69, G 0.69, H 0.38, I 0.64, J 0.00 (1 row). By holiday group: Easter 0.65, fixed dates 0.70, lunar 0.78, nth weekdays 0.66. The weakest family is H, near misses ("last minute christmas shopping", a song title).

Size (eval.ts gzip): 29.2 kB eager (+0.05 kB against h16), 51.0 kB lazy (+1.4 kB). Median latency 0.41 ms (h16 0.40 ms).

Tests: `packages/baselines/test/h17.test.ts` (53 phrases and resolution on the reference day). `packages/core/test/core.test.ts` covers the past marker and counts on each form, including today counting as the most recent and Feb 29 counted back.

## Not covered

- Relative years ("purim last year", "christmas next year"): `x`. They count calendar years, not occurrences. Exact dates have the same gap.
- Near misses that read as a past holiday: "last minute christmas shopping" and "last christmas" as a song title (family H, 0.38).
- Older-engine readings that h17 keeps: "before last christmas" (h16 gives `12-25`), "from last cny until now" (h16 drops "until now"), "up to last labor day" and "ever since last anzac day" (h16 gives `x`).
- "next X" on the holiday itself is today ("next christmas" on Dec 25), as for "next dec 25".
