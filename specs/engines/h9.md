# h9: rules in front of h8 for named dates, nth weekday of a relative month, weekday with day of month, bounded recurrences and lone hour words.

## Method

- Engine `packages/baselines/src/h9.ts` (tiny, eager). Rules in `src/h9/rules.ts`, `src/h9/lang.ts`, `src/h9/all.ts`: a lazy chunk loaded by `loadH9()` (also loads the h7 v2 layer). Without it `parseH9` is h8. h8, h7 are called unchanged; nothing is copied.
- Order: named dates and holidays -> clean-up (tags, product codes, noise words, `N-M` followed by a time) -> typo normalizer of h8 -> foreign month names -> rules -> h8 -> post-fix (end year copied from the start).
- New DSL forms used: `WD#K@M±N`, `b#K@M±N`, `WD&DNN` (`specs/dsl.md`, "Nth weekday of a relative month, weekday with a day of month").
- Error analysis: `scripts/pools.ts <engine>` scores every open pool (17 pools plus the clean typo-dev sources) and writes predictions and failures. No sealed data, `evals/e1..e11` or `test*` files were read. `data/pool/misc/dev_families.jsonl` came late and was used for tuning the families rules.

## Rules

1. Named dates: ides of a month (15th in Mar, May, Jul, Oct; else 13th), pi day, leap day (`02-29`, also with a time), a small holiday table (bastille day, australia day, guy fawkes or bonfire night, armistice day, st davids day, st stephens day, national day of germany, anzac day, ...).
2. Weekday with day of month: "(the) next/soonest/first/upcoming X (the) Nth", "X that is the Nth", "X the Nth this month", "friday the 13th" -> `wd&DNN`, with an optional time.
3. "(the) first/last/second ... X of this/next/last/the month" -> `wd#K@M±N`; "first/last weekday or business day of ..." -> `b#K@M±N`. "of the month" with a time only when the rest is a plain clock time. "every last X" -> `R m/1 wd#-1`.
4. Ranges: "the first of next month until the 15th" -> `D01@M+1>D15@M+1`; "from the 3rd to the 9th", "the 8th until the end of the month", "from friday until the end of the month", "from now until the end of the week", "through the end of next week" (open end), "this weekend through monday", "sat to the following wed" (`sa>we`). Skipped when either side has a month (h8 already gives a better result).
5. Recurrence bounds: "from A to B" (both bounds), "until the 20th" (`uD@20`), "until the end of the month" (`uM+0`, drops the `s` bound h8 adds), "for the rest of the week", "starting the 5th, 4 times", counts as occurrences for "every other day for 12 days" (`n6`), "every 2 days for a week", nth lists ("first and third mondays ... for 6 months" -> `n12`). "business/working days" are weekdays. "weekdays but skip friday" and "all week except sunday" are weekday sets.
6. Lone hour words: the whole input is one..twelve (typos by edit distance 1 for words of 5+ letters, a few aliases), optionally "o'clock"/"oclock" -> `t N?`. Digits are unchanged (h8 and h9 give `x` for 0, 13..23, 24; checked). Words in sentences, `no`, `to`, `fine`, `tent`, `eighth`, `ten percent` are unchanged.
7. Small forms: "N minutes to/past H" with number words ("twenty-eight minutes to seven" -> `t 6:32?`), "every 2 fridays" -> `w/2`, "monday to saturdays", "jog weekly" -> `R w/1`, CJK dates, `every 3 weeks on` -> `x` (dangling "on").
8. Chained offsets: "five days after the day after tomorrow" -> `+2d~+5d`; "the week after next" -> `W+2`; "the sunday before last" -> `su-2`; "one week before the 1st of next month" -> `D01@M+1~-1w`.
9. Two dated points joined by "ends", "departure", "dropoff", "return", ... -> a range only when both sides have a month. Copy the start year to the end ("jan 3rd 2027 till jan 10th" -> `2027-01-03>2027-01-10`) for `d` ranges.
10. Foreign month and weekday names (fr, es, de, it, pt, nl, ro, fi) -> English before h8. Connector words (`de`, `le`, `klo`) dropped only when a name was found.
11. Noise: `@tag`, `#tag`, priority `p1` (with `@` or `#`), product codes (`a380`), "room 12", "form 1040", "the 2nd floor", "3 x 4", "2 for 1", "morning of" are removed before parsing.
12. Guard: `dt X >T` (a space before `>`, an h8 bug in `withRest`) becomes `d X`.

## Cost

- Eager gzip 21.93 kB (h8 21.87 kB). Lazy 16.35 kB (h8 10.68 kB, so +5.7 kB lazy). Node latency on e10: p50 0.13 ms, p95 0.71 ms, p99 1.8 ms (h8 p95 about 0.56). Not measured on a phone.
- `validate-data.ts` on h9 predictions for all 65,749 pool texts: 0 bad (h8 had one: `in three months 3 till 5 pm` -> `dt D03@M+3 >17:00`, fixed by the guard).

## Label conflicts and suspected wrong labels (labels not edited)

- `3 weeks from tomorrow` (web dev `r-web...`, typo dev, tax, real train: `d +22d`, `d +11d`, `d +8d`, `d +15d`) vs `dev_families` `d +1d~+3w`. h9 follows the old labels (the rule fires only for "the day after tomorrow"). 9 rows are affected.
- `the 15th of this month` and `the 30th of this month` (v2 train, web dev4): label `D@15`, h9 gives `D15@M+0` (from h8).
- `first/last X of the month` (v007: 4 sources, about 25 texts and duplicates): label `R m/1 wd#K`; `dev_families` and the request label a single date (`mo#2@M+0`). h9 gives the single date. The conflict costs v007 rows (the 'new' failures on v007 are almost all this pattern plus the lone number words); v007 is still higher than h8 overall.
- `every last X`, `every last thursday` (v007): label `x`; h9 gives `R m/1 th#-1`. Looks wrong.
- Lone number words (`one`, `two`, `three`, ... in v007, about 25 rows): label `x`; h9 gives `t N?` as requested.
- `every 4 days for 10 days` (v007: `n10`) vs `dev_families` `every other day for 12 days` (`n6`). h9 uses the occurrence count only for "every other/second" and unit changes.
- `fri the 13th` (real-hard dev): `d 11-13` (a concrete date) vs `fr&D13`. h9 requires the full word `friday`.
- `every 2nd wed` (real-hard): `R m/1 we#2` conflicts with the policy in `specs/dsl.md` (`R w/2 we`). `weekly on tuesday and thursday for five weeks` (web dev): `u+5w`; similar texts elsewhere use `n`.
- `the day before Christmas 2006` `d 2006-12-24`, `two days after the 1st of december` `d 12-03`, `the day before christmas eve` `d 12-23`: concrete dates where similar rows use `~`.
- `dec 1 until the 23rd, mondays through fridays 9 to 5` and `2 for 1`-like rows: fine. `5:45 am to 6am` -> `t 05:45>06:15` and `6am through 7:15 am` -> `06:30>07:15` (v007): label noise.
- `ferry ... through september` `u09-30` vs tax `until june` `uM@06`: two label styles for the same meaning (also `through january` `u01-31`).
- `10 till noon` (real train) `t 10:00>12:00` vs the policy `10?>12:00`.
- `Payment received. Your order ships within 2 business days.` (real-hard) `x`; `a score of years ago` `x` (real-fmt) vs a hand-written row `d -20y`.

## Remaining clusters

- "by X" policy (largest single cluster; one rule once decided).
- Weekday-letter lists with a time (`tth 9am`): recurrence or list.
- `every morning this week at 6am` (`dt R d/1 uW+0 06:00 sW+0`), period bounds on recurrences with a day part.
- Roman-numeral and other foreign formats (`31.xii.99`, `8/Oct/2012`).
- `10 after 2`, `10 till 2`-style minute-to-hour forms with digits.
- Quarter and week-of phrases (`first to last day of 2008`).
- Partial typing: v007 labels a recurrence that ends in a dangling word as `x` in some forms and as the committed part in others.
