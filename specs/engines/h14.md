# h14: a date range with one shared time window, in front of h13.

## Method

- Engine `packages/baselines/src/h14.ts`. The rule is a lazy chunk (`src/h14/shared-time.ts`, loaded by `loadH14()` after h13's chunks). Without `loadH14`, `parseH14` is h12. h1 to h13 are unchanged.
- Problem (reported in the picker): "tomorrow to friday 3-4pm" gave `d +1d>fr` (time dropped) and "3-4pm tomorrow to friday" gave `dt +1d 15:00>16:00` (end day dropped). The same with absolute dates ("dec 1 to dec 5 3-4pm"). The label policy had no rule for this shape, and no open pool row used it.
- Policy (`specs/dsl.md`, "Date range with a shared time"): a range of two single days with one shared time is a daily recurrence bounded by the days, `dt R d/1 ufr 15:00>16:00 s+1d`. A time on each end ("3pm tomorrow to 4pm friday") stays one span.
- Rule: runs only when h13's whole-text answer is a bare day range or a single day with a time. The text is split once at a space into a date part and a time part (either order), and each is parsed by h13. It fires when:
  - the date part has a range word (to, through, until, a dash, between...and), no time of its own (clock time, noon, morning, evening, ...), and parses to `d A>B` with two single days (not W, M, Y, Q periods);
  - the time part has a clear time (a colon, am/pm, noon, midnight, or "at" and a number), holds only time words or a zone h13 reads, and parses to `t X` or `t X>Y`.
- The output goes through the core serializer for canonical token order. A zone in the time part stays the last token.
- Picker: the built-in parser is h14. The recurrence summary now names a start bound ("Every day at 3:00 - 4:00 PM from Nov 22 until Nov 27, 2026").

## Results

No sealed set was cut for this rule. It covers one shape reported by the user, and no open or eval row has that shape, so the numbers below show only that nothing else moved.

- Regression gate (`scripts/pools.ts h14 --compare h13`): 68,859 open pool rows, 0 regressions. One row changes: "from the 5th to the 10th at 9am" (`r-fam0193`) is labeled as a span, `dt D@05 09:00>D@10`, which conflicts with the new policy. It is listed in `h14-known-conflicts.json` with h13's conflicts.
- First gate run found 5 more regressions, all fixed before the numbers above: a bare month parsed as a day range ("16:30 4 jul 2025", 16 v007 rows), "between 1 and 12 july 2026", a time on the end day ("6am dec 5 to 7am", "from noon friday until monday morning"), and a sentence as the time part ("Nov 27-29 (check-in 4pm, check-out noon)").
- e16: exact 0.9253, resolved 0.9289, the same as h13 in aggregate. Against h13 it fixes one row (`test_tz_writers`), breaks one (`test_families`) and gives a different wrong answer on one (`test_public`). The rows were not read: the evals are held out, and the rule was not tuned on them. Every eval version e1 to e16 differs from h13 by at most these three rows (`runs/h14`).
- Size: 22.3 kB eager (+80 B against h13), 43.5 kB lazy (+3.5 kB, mostly the DSL parser and serializer the rule uses). Median latency 0.34 ms, unchanged.
- Tests: `packages/baselines/test/h14.test.ts` (22 phrases, and a check that the recurrence covers the same days as the date range for three values of today).

## Not covered

- "every day tomorrow to friday 3-4pm" gives `d R d/1` (h13 drops the bounds and the time). h14 leaves recurrences alone.
- A bare hour as the time ("tomorrow to friday at 3" works, "tomorrow to friday 3" does not). A bare number is too often a year or a day.
